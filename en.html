Privacy Policy

Lab Scope – Oscilloscope and Tuner

Last updated: 15 October 2026

In short

Lab Scope analyses the microphone only on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking services and no servers. Lab Scope does not transmit any data to us or to third parties.

Controller

Peter Seitz, email: labscope@peter-seitz.net

Microphone

Lab Scope needs microphone access to show the waveform and pitch live. The signal is processed only in your device's memory and is not transmitted. You can revoke access at any time in the iOS settings.

Recall

So that you can play, edit or compare the last seconds again, Lab Scope keeps a buffer in memory (adjustable from 0 to 300 seconds, 0 = off). The buffer is not stored permanently and is gone as soon as the app quits.

Videos and audio files

When you create a video or a WAV file, Lab Scope places it temporarily in the app's cache on your device so that you can share it. Lab Scope deletes these files automatically when you close Recall, at the latest the next time the app starts.

Whatever you pass on via the share menu (for example to Messages, Mail, Files or another app) is processed by the chosen app or recipient under their own terms.

Settings

Lab Scope stores your settings (notation, concert pitch, buffer, threshold) only locally on your device.

Support by email

If you write to labscope@peter-seitz.net, we use your message and email address only to reply to you and do not pass them on.

Your rights

As Lab Scope does not transmit any personal data to us, we hold no data about you apart from support emails. For support emails you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection under the GDPR, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Changes

If the app changes in a way that processes other data, we will update this policy beforehand.
